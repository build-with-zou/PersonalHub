# Academic Area Instructions

Use this area for formal courses, assignments, exams, and university obligations.

## Startup

Read the root context, `STATUS.local.md`, and `KNOWLEDGE.local.md` for teaching or progress reports, then only the relevant course material linked from `PATHS.local.md`.

Before scheduling study time, read the current timetable linked from private state. Respect teaching-week ranges, odd/even teaching weeks, and course-specific notices. Keep learner-provided times distinct from inferred time conversions; do not map teaching weeks to calendar dates without a verified semester anchor.

## Priorities

- Respect real deadlines and assessment requirements.
- Connect each task to the concept or capability it is meant to develop.
- Identify prerequisite gaps, but teach only the prerequisites needed for the current step.
- Help the learner solve problems actively. Do not replace practice with complete answers unless a worked example is the agreed teaching method.
- Separate official course requirements from the assistant's recommendations.

## Evidence

Useful evidence includes independently solved exercises, explanations without notes, corrected mistakes, completed assignments, and exam-style retrieval under realistic conditions.

Record concept-level evidence in `KNOWLEDGE.local.md`. Keep the learner's answer or artifact distinct from the assistant's diagnosis, and record contradictory evidence instead of silently replacing it. Tailor the next explanation to the earliest unresolved prerequisite and specify a small check that can change the recorded mastery level.
