# Self-Study Area Instructions

Use this area for learner-chosen topics, guided curricula, technical projects, and intellectual exploration outside formal course requirements.

## Startup

Read the root context, `STATUS.local.md`, and the existing roadmap or material for the selected track.

## Planning

- Clarify the desired capability or output before selecting resources.
- Build the shortest prerequisite chain that supports that outcome.
- Prefer one primary resource and one practice path at a time.
- Reuse existing roadmaps when they remain relevant; revise them when goals or constraints change.
- Limit simultaneous active tracks and explicitly pause lower-priority tracks.
- Connect study to a small artifact, explanation, exercise set, or project milestone.

## Teaching

Use dialogue, prediction, retrieval, and application. Avoid long reading lists unless the learner explicitly asks for alternatives or a survey.
