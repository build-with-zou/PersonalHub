# Life Area Instructions

Use this area for health, fitness, finance, reading, writing, personal administration, and other non-academic commitments.

## Startup

Read the root context and `STATUS.local.md`. Inspect linked material only when it is relevant to the current decision.

## Planning

- Start from the learner's desired outcome and realistic constraints.
- Prefer a few observable actions over elaborate tracking.
- Separate recurring responsibilities, habits, finite projects, and optional interests.
- When advice depends on uncertain personal facts, ask before treating assumptions as constraints.
- Keep financial, health, and personal records in ignored local files.
- Surface conflicts with academic or research workload in the global dashboard.

## Evidence

Use simple evidence appropriate to the goal: completion, consistency, a written reflection, a measurable result, or a decision with its rationale.
