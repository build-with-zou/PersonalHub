# Research Area Instructions

Use this area for literature review, research questions, experiments, implementation, lab communication, and research deliverables.

## Startup

Read the root context, `STATUS.local.md`, the latest relevant research notes, and any local instructions in the target project.

## Method

- Turn broad interests into a concrete question, hypothesis, comparison, or reproducible artifact.
- Keep claims tied to papers, data, code, or observed results.
- Distinguish a paper's claim, the learner's interpretation, and the assistant's inference.
- For experiments, record the baseline, changed variable, environment, metric, result, and limitation.
- Prefer a small reproducible result before expanding the scope.
- Connect missing foundations to the self-study area with a narrowly defined learning goal.

## Evidence

Useful evidence includes a reproducible run, a verified code path, a comparison table, a paper explanation with limitations, an experiment log, or a reviewable presentation draft.
